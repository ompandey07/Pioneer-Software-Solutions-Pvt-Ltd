Core Architecture

What Is Oval9?

Oval9 is a web-based business management software designed specifically for businesses operating in Nepal. Unlike generic ERP solutions built for foreign markets, Oval9 is engineered from the ground up to meet the unique needs of Nepali businesses — including full compliance with the Inland Revenue Department's requirements, native support for the Bikram Sambat calendar, and real-time synchronisation with the government's Computerised Billing Management System (CBMS) for VAT purposes.

The platform supports multi-company operations from a single instance, making it ideal for business groups and holding companies that need to manage multiple entities without switching between different systems. Each company's data is fully isolated through a strict tenant architecture, ensuring complete privacy, security, and data integrity across all accounts.

A Platform Built Around 16 Powerful Modules

Oval9 is organised into sixteen independent application modules, each handling a distinct area of your business. Together, they form a seamless, fully integrated system where data flows automatically from one module to the next — eliminating duplication, reducing errors, and giving you a real-time view of your entire operation.

Trading & Sales

Create and manage sales invoices, quotations, and orders with speed and accuracy across wholesale and retail streams.

Purchase Management

Streamline your procurement process end-to-end from purchase requisitions and orders to supplier payments.

Inventory Control

Track stock levels in real time, manage multiple warehouses, batch expiry dates, and never run out of best-selling products.

Finance & Accounting

Maintain accurate financial books with journal entries, general ledgers, trial balances, and automated financial statements.

Payments & Receipts

Record every cash, cheque, and digital transaction with full audit trails and automated bank reconciliation support.

VAT & Tax Reporting

Generate government-ready VAT returns including Anusuchi 13, TDS schedules, and other IRD-compliant filings automatically.

Customer Management

Maintain a complete database of your clients, credit limits, transaction histories, and outstanding receivable balances.

Supplier Management

Keep detailed records of all vendors, purchase agreements, order histories, and scheduled payable balances in one place.

Export Module

Handle international trade documentation, customs manifest filings, and foreign currency LC requirements effortlessly.

Travel Management

Manage business travel booking requests, passenger itineraries, expense claims, and employee reimbursements efficiently.

Law Firm Module

Specialised suite for legal practices to manage client case files, court schedules, retainer billing, and legal documentation.

Audit Management

Maintain strict transparency and internal compliance with immutable user activity logs and built-in audit trail tools.

Reports & Analytics

Access over 100 pre-built reports covering every operational corner of your enterprise with multi-format export capabilities.

AI Assistant

Ask operational questions in plain English and receive instant, data-backed analytical insights directly from your live database.

Custom Print Formats

Design and generate professional invoices, thermal receipts, and official tax documents styled in your custom branding.

Smart Dashboard

Reporting Engine

100+ Reports, Always Ready

One of Oval9's most powerful features is its reporting engine. With over 100 pre-built reports available across more than 15 export formats, you always have the information you need — in the format you need it.
